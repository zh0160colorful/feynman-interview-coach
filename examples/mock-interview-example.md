# Mock Interview Example

## Scenario

Candidate is preparing for a product manager interview.

## Coach

Tell me about a product decision you made.

## Candidate

I improved the onboarding flow.

## Coach

What problem were you solving?

## Candidate

Users were dropping during registration.

## Coach

How did you know this was the key problem? What evidence supported your decision?

## Training Goal

The coach does not accept feature descriptions alone. It guides candidates from:

Feature → Problem → Evidence → Decision → Trade-off → Result
