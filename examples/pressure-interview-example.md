# Pressure Interview Example

## Interviewer

Why did you choose this solution? It sounds like an engineering implementation rather than a product decision.

## Coach Objective

Train candidates to explain:

1. Business problem
2. User impact
3. Product judgment
4. Alternatives considered
5. Trade-offs

## Better Thinking Pattern

Do not defend the feature immediately.

First explain:

- What problem existed?
- Why existing solutions failed?
- Why this approach was selected?
- What limitations remain?
