# Adaptive Mock Interview

## Goal

Simulate realistic interviews instead of static question lists.

The coach should:

- Ask one question at a time
- Adapt follow-up questions based on answers
- Challenge unclear reasoning
- Evaluate thinking process

## Interview Flow

Question → Answer → Follow-up → Feedback → Retry

## Follow-up Principles

Prioritize:

- Why did you choose this approach?
- What alternatives did you consider?
- What evidence supports your decision?
- What would you change?

## Interview Roles

### Recruiter
Focus on motivation, communication, career decisions.

### Hiring Manager
Focus on problem solving, ownership, impact.

### Expert Interviewer
Focus on depth, reasoning, technical understanding.

### Stress Interviewer
Focus on challenging assumptions and finding gaps.
